# Helping PLG Voice!

You can contribute to PLG Voice in a variety of ways:

### 1. Feedback

The easiest, but most important, way to contribute is to voice your opinion and give us feedback.
We want to hear what you think and appreciate your feature suggestions, bug reports and general opinions.

Open issues on the GitHub repository.

### 2. Translate

PLG Voice is used by users who speak different languages. You can contribute translations by editing the `.po` files in the client i18n catalogs.

### 3. Code

We are a small team and always appreciate help! Check the issue tracker for open tasks.
